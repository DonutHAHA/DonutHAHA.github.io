About Donut

Donut is a Scottish Fold with a face that has launched a thousand memes. Born to nap and raised on cuddles, she spends roughly twenty-two hours a day testing the structural limits of pillows, blankets, and the occasional narrow shelf. Her official job title is Chief Vibes Officer of the household, a role she performs almost entirely while unconscious.

Despite her permanently grumpy resting face, Donut is famously easygoing — she'll let you balance nearly anything on her head, cuddle her favorite fish plushie in public, and pose for photos mid-yawn without a single complaint. Critics call her "unbothered." Fans call her an icon.

Interesting Facts

🐾

Folded Royalty

Those adorable folded-down ears aren't a hairstyle — they're a genetic trait of the Scottish Fold breed, and they make Donut look permanently, delightfully skeptical.

🐟

Emotional Support Fish

Donut has a beloved yellow fabric fish toy that she naps next to almost every single day. It's basically her co-star.

🙃

Gravity-Defying Napper

Donut sleeps in positions that seem physically inadvisable — fully upside down, paws in the air, wedged into a narrow shelf, you name it.

🎩

Bottomless Patience

She has been photographed calmly balancing a lint roller cap on her head without moving a muscle. A true professional.

😾

The Resting Grump

Donut's default face reads as "mildly disappointed in you," even when she's actually perfectly content. It's an art form.

☀️

Sunbeam Connoisseur

Given the choice between any spot in the house, Donut will always pick the warmest, sunniest patch of blanket available.
